* Stage 5: <<gitGroup Consistency>> --- the Same Conventions Across a gitGroup's Repos

<<Stage 5>>

Goal: every repo of the bisos-pip gitGroup follows the same conventions, checked by a tool rather
than remembered. First conventions: the root =.gitignore= and =.gitattributes=.

Decided (user, 2026-11-15):
- Build it general, for any gitGroup (option C): one engine in *bisos.gitist*; each gitGroup declares
  its own rules.
- A gitGroup's rules live in its [[overview repo]] (here, for bisos-pip).
- =py3/README.rst= and =py3/_description.org= are generated, so they belong in =.gitignore= (as the
  template has said since 2026-07-12) and should not be tracked.
- The pattern is =pipPkgsStatus='s: a read-only status per repo and rule, with the action it implies,
  then an explicit update that applies only the safe actions.

  2026-07-12, one each of 2026-09-17 and 2023-04-19); 44 share one copy matching no version in the
  =defaults= history (likely a template from before it moved there); about 5 are unique; 16 have none.
- =.gitattributes= (template =gitattributes/py=): 59 equal it, 17 have none.
- The 44 still ignore =*_archive=, so a future =AI-WorkPlan.org_archive= there would be untracked
  (template changed 2026-09-17; today's archives, in aiActivity and airflow, are tracked).
- 57 repos track =py3/README.rst=, against the template.
- =cbmPkgScaffold.sh= (cbm-package-authorship skill) copies =py3/.gitignore= and =py3/.gitattributes=
